// 001 — who we are

a new kind of institution

amaze consortium is a next-generation youth-led non-profit, structured the way brac or grameen would build a family of purpose-driven ventures — a parent organization overseeing distinct, independently branded subsidiaries. we are not a traditional institution. we are an ecosystem — a structured network of subsidiaries, each targeting a distinct domain, all connected by a singular drive to create impact at scale. every subsidiary is staffed, in large part, by students we've trained ourselves. through the amaze apprenticeship, members get real hands-on experience before earning a role inside one of our ventures.

we train talent. we build institutions. we define the future.

// 003 — leadership
"the world was not made for you, you were made for it."

amaze consortium was built on a belief that the next generation of leaders doesn't fit neatly into existing systems. it was built for those who think differently, act deliberately, and build with purpose.

ariyan biswas is a bangladeshi entrepreneur, designer, and organizational leader who founded amaze consortium and was the former ceo at arcio with a focus on innovation, education, media, strategy, and community-driven development through modern digital ecosystems.
